Per-frame cost with the shipped config (ms): wait/camera ~8, hands ~20-35, segment ~6 (avg), vcam ~6, preview ~9.

process lands on slower cores. 120 s run: 16.9 FPS average. The >= 24 FPS phase-2 target is NOT met.
